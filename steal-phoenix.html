How to Steal the Phoenix in GTA 6 (Unwanted Attention)

Unwanted Attention is an early Chapter 1 mission where Jason must steal a red Imponte '77 Phoenix out of the police impound in Hamlet. It teaches car theft and police evasion — and stealth makes it far easier than a shootout.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Start the mission

Meet Cal at his fishing spot in Corsair Key, north of Key Lento — mark his location from your text messages. Brian has tasked Jason with recovering the Phoenix: a grow-house owner left about 20 pounds of weed in its trunk, and the car ended up in the police impound. Get in Cal's truck and drive to Hamlet with him.

Get into the Hamlet police impound

The Phoenix is hooked to a tow truck at the back of the impound, under a carport at the northeast end. There are two patrolling guards whose routes don't overlap. Three ways in:

  • Scissor lift (easiest). It stands against the impound wall to the right of the building. Ride it to the roof, walk across and drop into the back lot.
  • Hole in the barbed wire. Hug the left side of the outer wall until you find an opening in the fence.
  • Fence and containers. Climb the fence, then the containers in the back corner to drop over the second fence.

Stay crouched and use Focus (click both sticks) to highlight the guards' locations. Stealth keeps your criminal profile clean — a knocked-out guard brings a police patrol, so sneaking past is the better option if you want to stay Professional.

Steal the Phoenix

  1. Use the WAiNK app to identify the car — the lot is full of red cars, and the right one shows Imponte '77 Phoenix when you get close. It's the one hanging off the blue tow truck.
  2. Before taking the car, enter the building behind it and silently take down the guard manning the gate, then press the button on the wall to open the gate.
  3. Lower the Phoenix off the tow truck using the tow truck's controls.
  4. Hotwire it: move both sticks to the marked spots until the two semi-circles fill.

Lose the cops and deliver the car

  1. Drive out through the open gate. You'll become Wanted — stick to backstreets and off-road routes and get out of the flashing search area on the minimap.
  2. Head to the Pay 'n' Spray in Hamlet and respray the Phoenix (any paint works) so the police stop recognising it.
  3. Deliver the car to Brian's garage and collect the cash in the back room.
  4. Change your appearance: take off your hat and glasses on the loadout wheel (the step only finishes when the wheel closes), then buy a new outfit at the nearby gun store. That clears the heat.

Reward: $150. Unlocks: the police scanner app on your phone (from Cal, so you hear police radio during a chase) and the WAiNK app, which scans cars. New on screen after this mission: icons showing what the police recognise about you — your clothes, hat and glasses, and your car.
