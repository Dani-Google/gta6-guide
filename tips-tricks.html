GTA 6 Tips & Tricks: 12 Things to Know First

GTA 6 doesn't explain half of what it's doing. These spoiler-free tips will save you cash, time and frustration in your first hours in Leonida.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. Bank your cash — you lose what you carry

Cash on you is lost when you die (Wasted) or get arrested (Busted). Early in Chapter 1, BuckMe sets Jason up with a bank account — put cash in it every time you come back from a job. The bank balance is shared between Jason and Lucia. Before you get bank access, there's a hidden cash stash in the floor of Jason's bedroom.

2. There is no traditional difficulty — but there are assists

GTA 6 has no difficulty options, but you can make Leonida more manageable: press Options, go to Settings > Gameplay, scroll to the bottom (options 24 and 25) and enable Accessible Combat (less damage taken, more damage dealt) and Reduced Vehicle Damage.

3. Switch to Classic controls if GTA 5 is in your muscle memory

The default scheme maps run to L3 and jump to Cross/X. If you spent a decade on GTA 5, go to Settings > Control Scheme and toggle to Classic. See our full GTA 6 controls guide.

4. Use Focus all the time

Click both sticks to use Focus: the world slows down and lights up. In combat it slows time; outside a fight it stays on as long as you want — enemies glow red, usable objects glow yellow, and cash glows green. It's also how you spot safe codes, fish, and guards' positions.

5. Heal from the weapon wheel

Hold L1 (PS5) / LB (Xbox) for the weapon wheel, then press Square / X to heal with Zombix. Healing items show in the bottom-left corner of the wheel. See how to heal and raise max health.

6. Steal cars faster with the sweet spot

When a criminal minigame appears — Slim Jim, Clone Key, or hotwiring — there's a pink "sweet spot". Move the sticks around to find it and the action speeds up, giving you extra seconds to escape.

7. Find your next mission on the map

Press the Touch Pad to open your map, then use the index on the left and press Right to see available story missions — mandatory objectives at the top, optional ones below. Set a waypoint with Cross/X.

8. The HUD hides itself — press the D-pad

With the default setting, the HUD fades away. Press the D-pad to bring it back and see your current objective. In a car, hold the D-pad for radio and vehicle controls.

9. Wear a hat or mask on every job

Police remember your face, clothes, car and whether your partner was with you — each shows as a red icon under the wanted stars. Go in with a hat or bandana, take it off after, keep a change of clothes in the car, and put your gun away before anyone sees it. See how to lose the cops.

10. Scan cars with WAiNK before you steal them

Cal puts the WAiNK app on your phone during Unwanted Attention. Hold up on the D-pad, point it at any car, and see its security, tracker, sell value and registration cost. Scan every new model you pass — the 100% checklist wants 200 of them.

11. Make a manual save at the start of each chapter

There is no mission replay in GTA 6. Save manually as each chapter begins so you can go back if you miss something.

12. Settle in — the opening is long

The first stretch is a scripted prologue of cinematics, credits and story missions. Free roam starts about 46 minutes in, when Jason arrives at his house. Use the time to learn the controls — and explore the street you start on in Key Lento: a dive bar, tattoo parlour, gift shops, a 24/7 store, a Marbles burger restaurant and a mod shop (Coastal Auto Care).
