GTA 6 Story Missions List

The GTA 6 main story has 91 missions. Chapter 1 alone has 22 of them. This page lists the full Chapter 1 order as printed in the game's Story Recap — two walkthroughs (PowerPyx and VGC) agree on it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

No mission replay. Unlike past GTA games, GTA 6 missions do not have Gold Medal objectives, and there is no replaying missions. Make a manual save as each chapter begins.

How long is the GTA 6 story?

Early guides put the main story at roughly 50-odd hours, with 100% completion taking around 100 hours more on top. The story is split across chapters — Chapter 2 opens up co-op play between Jason and Lucia after the Coffee Break mission, and bigger robberies get much easier once both characters can play together.

Chapter 1 missables to know about

  • Recalibration is a point of no return. After it, Jason's house in Key Lento is gone for good, and the pocket cash you carry in is lost. Make a separate manual save beforehand and put at least $5,000 in Family Savings first.
  • Two optional tasks are missable: Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter.
  • Romance head start: before Lucia arrives, fix the house, buy flowers and collect her clothes — see our romance guide.
  • Meditation: after the Down to Earth mission, look for Lucia at Jason's house in the morning. When she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept — complete it before leaving Chapter 1.
