GTA 6 Trailer 3: The Launch Trailer Explained

There is no official video called "GTA 6 Trailer 3" — but everyone searching for one means the same thing: the 90-second Launch Trailer Rockstar dropped on 15 October 2026. Here is what it showed, and why it matters.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The confusion: there is no "Trailer 3"

Rockstar never released a video with the name "Trailer 3". The official video list on Rockstar's GTA VI site shows only Trailer 1, Trailer 2, An Extended Look, the Launch Trailer and Out Now. When people search for "GTA 6 Trailer 3", they almost always mean the Launch Trailer from 15 October 2026 — the third traditional story trailer, because An Extended Look was a long gameplay showcase rather than a trailer.

Every official GTA 6 video, in order

  • Trailer 1 — 4 December 2023 (91 seconds): introduced Lucia, Vice City and the tone of the whole game.
  • Trailer 2 — 6 May 2025: the Jason and Lucia story trailer — the one fans have been mining for details ever since.
  • An Extended Look — 27 August 2026 (27 minutes): a long-form gameplay showcase, every frame captured on PS5. It premiered first on Netflix (3:00 PM ET) and landed on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the GTA VI site the same evening. A tower-block shootout, a getaway with Jason firing from the back seat while Lucia drives, and a quiet evening at home.
  • Launch Trailer (the so-called Trailer 3) — 15 October 2026 (90 seconds): the final trailer before release. Details below.
  • Out Now — 19 November 2026: the launch-day video.

What the Launch Trailer showed

The Launch Trailer runs 90 seconds, was captured entirely in-game on a PlayStation 5, and pulled 37.9 million views on Rockstar's main YouTube channel in its first two days (plus another 9.9 million on the UK & Ireland channel). Jason narrates it. The biggest confirmed reveals:

  • Jason's army past: "I got in trouble in the army. Didn't leave on the best of terms." — the first confirmation that Jason is a former soldier, which shapes the story.
  • A bank vault heist — the centrepiece action sequence.
  • A helicopter cargo-net mission — Lucia hangs from a helicopter, leaping from a speeding car onto a moving aircraft and using a grenade launcher to destroy a SWAT barricade.
  • Jason & Lucia's apartment — the couple's home base, shown in a quiet scene.
  • A DacityCoin crypto biplane — a crypto-themed aircraft, one of the trailer's stranger details.
  • A swamp mission in tactical gear — Jason and two allies emerge from a swamp wearing full tactical gear and night-vision optics, pointing to military-style missions.
  • The romance front and centre: a shower scene, Lucia asking if they will be okay, Jason answering that they always stick together — and a final shot of the couple jumping from a balcony while holding hands.
  • Weapon customisation: Jason is seen adjusting a burst-fire switch on his rifle while fleeing across a roof — a gunplay option not previously offered in the series.
  • Everyday Leonida: NPCs on electric scooters, snakes, and people eating buckets of fried chicken.

In Rockstar's words, the trailer shows Jason and Lucia's "easy score" going wrong and dragging them into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida.

The song — and the score

The trailer is set to "Flower in a Hurricane" by Justice feat. Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. Rockstar confirmed alongside the trailer that Justice is composing the game's original score — the French duo is not just licensing one song. The track is on "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album", the official soundtrack.

How it differs from Trailers 1 and 2

  • Trailer 1 (Dec 2023) was a tone-setter: Lucia, Vice City, the vibe — 91 seconds, no story detail.
  • Trailer 2 (May 2025) was the story trailer: Jason and Lucia's relationship, the setup of the game.
  • The Launch Trailer (Oct 2026) is the action trailer: the most set pieces in the shortest time, the first confirmation of Jason's army background, and the first look at the game's original score. It is also the last trailer Rockstar released before launch — no further trailer has been announced.

Where to watch it

Watch the Launch Trailer on Rockstar Games' official YouTube channel, or on the official GTA VI site — search "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer". Do not trust re-uploads with "Trailer 3" in the title from other channels; the official upload is the one on Rockstar's own channel.
