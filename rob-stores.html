How to Rob Stores in GTA 6

You can rob almost any store in GTA 6 — and the map tells you how much each one should pay and how well it's guarded before you walk in. Here's how robberies work, and how to do them cleanly.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How to rob a store

  1. Check the map first. Each robbery target shows its expected payout and guard level before you walk in.
  2. Aim your gun at the clerk and loot the cash register.
  3. Find the safe. It's usually behind a locked door at the back. Look for the safe code on a note nearby — use Focus (click both sticks) to spot it before you reach for sticky bombs.
  4. Get out clean. The less violence, the better your criminal profile stays — and the less heat you carry afterwards.

Which stores pay best?

Hitting grocery stores won't make Jason and Lucia millionaires, but some robbery opportunities are extremely profitable — especially jewelry stores and banks. Jewelry heists open in Chapter 3 and pay up to $25,000; bank vault robberies at the end of Chapter 4 pay up to $205,000. For the full picture, see our how to make money fast guide.

Store robberies and 100% completion

Robberies are part of the 100% completion checklist: 34 of 35 Smash 'n' Grabs and 50 of 91 Stick Ups must be fully completed, along with 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs and 7 payday loan jobs. See the full 100% completion checklist.

Keep your profile clean. Robberies done quietly — back or side door, no detection, witnesses left alone — protect your criminal profile. Excess violence pushes you toward Violent, Psycho and eventually Irredeemable, which is permanent. See how to change your criminal profile.
