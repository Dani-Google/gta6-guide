GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist

Reaching 100% completion in GTA 6 unlocks the 'A Long Road' trophy/achievement — the hardest trophy in the game, and a step toward the Platinum. The checklist stays hidden until you finish the story, but everything you do before that still counts. Nothing is missable: every requirement can still be completed in free-roam after the story.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

View your progress in-game at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The good news: the requirements are lenient. Most tasks only need partial completion — the only ones that need everything are the robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's hunting quests.

Tips to reach 100% faster

  • Scan vehicles from the start. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app, including boats and aircraft — you'll reach 200 naturally during the story.
  • Study animals as you go. Look at any new animal through the binoculars.
  • Do optional missions as they appear. They count toward the 100 optional tasks, and their questlines introduce new features like fencing and bank jobs.
  • Don't collect absolutely everything. Many collectible types aren't required at all, and most side quests and strangers aren't needed.
  • Use the in-game tracker. Almost every requirement has its own counter in the Progress menu and a map filter, so you can see exactly what's left.
