GTA 6 Pre-Order: Editions, Prices & Bonuses

GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 — so the pre-order window is now closed. Here is what the two editions cost, what each one included, and what changed once the game was out.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short answer

Pre-orders opened on 25 June 2026 and closed at launch. The game costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The pre-order bonus — the Vintage Vice City Pack — was only for purchases made before 20 November 2026, so anyone buying now gets the game but not that pack. The prices have not changed since launch.

Editions and prices

  • Standard Edition — $79.99: the full base game. That is all of it.
  • Ultimate Edition — $99.99: the full base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade — 16 extra items threaded through Jason and Lucia's story, unlocking as you progress through the chapters.

There is no physical disc. Even the boxed copy sold in shops contains a download code, not a disc. The Ultimate Edition is digital only — a boxed copy bought from a shop is always the Standard Edition.

What the Ultimate Edition adds (16 items)

Rockstar lists 16 extras that only come with the Ultimate Edition:

  • '95 Grotti Cheetah — a mid-90s sports car
  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers — a matched pair of scoped revolvers
  • Personalized Weapon Variants — engraved sidearms for Jason's Girardi ES9 pistol and Lucia's Klose K17 pistol
  • Vice City Styles — exclusive outfits, tattoos and accessories
  • Jason's Safehouse Vehicles — an Army fatigue-tinged Dinka Enduro motorcycle and the Crest Kayak
  • Ganado Retro Build — a mod kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup
  • Rideout Customs Mod Shop — detailed interiors, rims and "donk stylings"
  • Sara's Unisex Salon — facial hair, makeup and nails for Jason and Lucia
  • Shitzu Squalo — a watercraft
  • Stock 305 Clothing Store — exclusive streetwear, including a furry spotted coat for Lucia
  • '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Garage
  • Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
  • One-Eyed Willie's Mod Shop
  • Goodtime Gear
  • PTT Youngin$ Compound — an exclusive mission/raid
  • Classic Car Collection — track down abandoned classic and project cars to restore for local fixer Wyman

None of these are available at the start — Rockstar says they are "threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story", with new items uncovered as you play through each chapter.

Pre-order bonuses (both editions)

Anyone who bought either edition before 20 November 2026 received the Vintage Vice City Pack:

  • '55 Vapid Stanier sedan in two-tone colours, with the Shorecourt Personal Garage — a beachfront property near Ocean Beach with a weapon locker and a stash point to fence stolen goods
  • Exclusive outfits and hairstyles for Jason (linen suit in vintage pastel) and Lucia (red sequin mini dress with curls)
  • Tommy Vercetti weapon pattern — the tropical shirt design from GTA Vice City as a gun wrap

Digital pre-orders also received one free month of GTA+, which adds $500,000 to your in-game bank balance and access to the GTA+ Games Library of classic Rockstar titles. Note that GTA+ is a subscription: it renews and charges you after the free month unless you cancel. Sony's store page says the free month must be claimed by 31 March 2027 and can be claimed once per account.

Can you upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later?

Yes. Rockstar confirmed that Standard Edition owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately through the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store at any time. So there is no penalty for starting with Standard and upgrading later if you decide you want the extras.

Is it still worth pre-ordering — or buying now?

Pre-ordering no longer applies: the game is out. The only thing that changed at launch is the Vintage Vice City Pack — it was a pre-order bonus, and it is no longer available to new buyers. Everything else is unchanged: same two editions, same prices, same Ultimate extras, and the same free upgrade path from Standard to Ultimate. If you mainly want the game, buy Standard. If you want the 16 extra vehicles, weapons, shops and missions, buy Ultimate — or start with Standard and upgrade later.
