GTA 6 Cheat Codes: All 19 Verified Codes

GTA 6 has 61 cheats in total. As of 20 November 2026, 19 codes are known — every one reported by outlets that played the released game (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, GameRant, PowerPyx, GameSpot, Polygon). The other 42 cheats have a name and a challenge, but no published code yet.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The catch: you must finish the story first. No cheat works until you complete the main story (91 missions). After the credits, a Cheats tab appears in the Progress menu, and each cheat must be unlocked by completing its own challenge. Challenges you already completed before unlocking cheats still count.

Using a cheat disables saving for the rest of your session — and trophies/achievements too. Progress made with cheats on is progress you cannot save.

How to enter cheat codes in GTA 6

  1. Finish the main story and unlock the cheat by completing its challenge.
  2. In free roam, press Up on the D-pad to open the in-game phone.
  3. Hold L1 + R1 on PS5 (LB + RB on Xbox). Note: PC Gamer writes "hold L1 and press R1" — if one method fails, try the other.
  4. Enter the D-pad sequence below. The effect activates as soon as a correct code goes in for an unlocked cheat.

Codes are the same on PS5 and Xbox — only the shoulder buttons change names.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes: the $250,000 code above. But it does not replace the grind — once any cheat is used, the game cannot be saved for the rest of the session. Quit, and the save you load is the one from before the cheat. For money that stays, see our how to make money fast in GTA 6 guide.

What about the cheat lists from before launch?

They were fake. Pre-launch lists repeated Grand Theft Auto V codes — some with the end cut off — and used triggers and face buttons. Every outlet that played the game describes the phone and the D-pad, nothing else. Rockstar itself has published no cheat list.

Code confidence: $250,000, Raise Heat Level, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force, Exploding Balls, Maibatsu Boomazoom, HVY Bulldozer, Cycle Weather and Dollhouse appear identically in four outlets (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, GameRant, PowerPyx). Body Armor, Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo, No Reload, Vehicle Force, MTL Pumper, Cataclysm and Faded Glory match across three. Drunk and Raise Wanted Level rest on fewer sources — Drunk's code is confirmed by Polygon and TrueAchievements; Raise Wanted Level's by GameSpot, Radio Times and TrueAchievements. One outlet (Radio Times) prints a different Max Ammo sequence; the version above is the four-outlet majority. We will update this page as more codes are verified.
