GTA 6 Trophies & Platinum Roadmap

Grand Theft Auto VI has 55 trophies. The Platinum — "Vice Got a Price!" — requires earning every other trophy in the base game. Here's the roadmap, and the trophies we can confirm so far.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Cheats disable trophies. Using any cheat code disables trophies and achievements for the rest of that play session — and blocks saving too. Never use cheats on a trophy run. See GTA 6 cheat codes.

Platinum roadmap

  1. Step 1 — Complete the story and important side activities (60–80 hours). Finish the main story and do optional missions as they become available, especially those introducing robberies, fishing, hunting and other activities. After the Down to Earth mission, look for Lucia at Jason's house in the morning — when she mentions meditating, follow her to the balcony and accept. Complete the balcony meditation before leaving Chapter 1.
  2. Step 2 — Reach 100% completion and finish challenges (around 100 hours). Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story. Complete all required robberies, collectibles, races, shooting range gold medals, drone time trials, stunts and Bret's hunting quests. See the full 100% completion checklist.
  3. Step 3 — Finish Strangers & Freaks and remaining trophies (10–20 hours). Complete the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and finish eight qualifying Strangers & Freaks mission strands across Leonida.

Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals through the binoculars from the start — you'll eventually need 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animal species, and collecting them naturally saves hours of cleanup.
