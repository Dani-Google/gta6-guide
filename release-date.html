GTA 6 Release Date, Price, Platforms & PC Version

After two delays and a 13-year wait since GTA 5, Grand Theft Auto VI is out. Here are the confirmed facts — and the rumours that are not true.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Release date

GTA 6 released on Thursday, 19 November 2026, playable from midnight local time. It was first announced in December 2023 for 2025, delayed on 2 May 2025 to 26 May 2026, and delayed again on 6 November 2025 to 19 November 2026. Pre-orders opened 25 June 2026, and digital pre-loading began 10 November 2026. The preload is up to 212 GB depending on the console.

Platforms

GTA 6 launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PlayStation 4 or Xbox One version.

Price

  • Standard Edition: $79.99
  • Ultimate Edition: $99.99

The game is not sold on a physical disc — it is available through digital downloads or download codes only.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No PC version has been announced. Rockstar has said PC players should follow official announcements for updates on a possible Windows version. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has said the company generally launches on platforms with meaningful audiences over time, and that PC is becoming more important — but that is not a PC release date.

GTA Online in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not officially confirmed an online mode yet. Reporting based on a Bloomberg video suggests multiplayer launches about a year after the single-player game, possibly in late 2027 — treat that as unconfirmed until Rockstar or Take-Two states it directly.

Launch by the numbers

  • 12 million pre-orders by 13 November 2026, worth $1.09 billion in presale revenue (Sensor Tower).
  • 517 million views across the official videos (Trailer 1 alone: 296 million).
  • Development cost estimated over $1 billion (Roth analyst Eric Handler).
  • The game returns to Vice City, in the wider US state of Leonida, with two protagonists, Jason and Lucia.
