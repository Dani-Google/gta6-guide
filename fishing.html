How to Fish in GTA 6: Key Lento Fishing Guide

Fishing is one of GTA 6's best early activities: it's unlocked immediately, it raises your Focus permanently, and catching all 35 fish species counts toward 100% completion. Here's how to start, right outside Jason's house in Key Lento.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How to start fishing in GTA 6

Fishing is unlocked as soon as you can move around freely as Jason — you don't need to wait for later chapters. The quickest first rod is on the small dock behind Jason's safehouse in Key Lento, where rods are lying around. You can also equip the rod from the weapon wheel anywhere near water:

  1. Move close to a body of water.
  2. Hold L1 on PS5 (LB on Xbox) to open the equipment wheel.
  3. Use the right stick to select the Equipment tab, then L2/R2 to cycle to the Fishing Rod.

The rod shares a slot with the binoculars and flashlight, and it is always in your inventory once you have it. The game also teaches fishing in Recalibration, a Chapter 1 mission where Jason takes Cal out on the boat.

How to cast, hook and reel in a fish

  1. Cast: hold L2 / Left Trigger to grip the rod, then press R2 / Right Trigger to throw the line. A longer wind-up sends the lure farther.
  2. Spot fish: press L3/R3 (or both sticks) to use Focus and see where fish are swimming.
  3. Hook: when a fish takes the bait, press R2 / Right Trigger to set the hook.
  4. Fight: push the left stick against the direction the fish is swimming to tire it out.
  5. Reel in: when the fish takes a break, rotate the right stick to reel it in.

If you overshoot your cast, press Circle (PS5) / B (Xbox) to bring the line back and try again. Dawn and dusk are the best hours for larger fish, and poor weather makes fishing harder, according to the in-game Help menu.

Lures: which one to use

You start with two lures: the Small Saltwater Lure and the Small Freshwater Lure. Each fish needs a particular lure, so buy the right one before you head out. Lures are sold at Ammu-Nation branches that stock fishing gear (look for the fish icon under Features on the map) and at Chuck's Tackle & Shot near the Key Lento safehouse, which sells the Medium Saltwater Lure.

Cal Hampton's fishing challenges

Cal Hampton, Jason's friend, is your fishing partner and rival. After the Recalibration mission, Cal texts you challenges — each puts a purple fish icon on your map marking where that species can be caught. The first is Catch a Bigger Fish: land a Stoplight Parrotfish heavier than 2lb in Gambit Bay. When you beat Cal's catch, take a photo and the game sends it to him automatically. Beating Cal raises your Focus and unlocks the Baited My Tackle trophy/achievement.

Why fish at all? Leonida holds 35 species of freshwater and saltwater fish. Catching all of them is part of the Animals Collection, which counts toward 100% completion. Fishing also raises Focus — and unlike Strength and Cardio, Focus gains are permanent. The Fisheye screen-filter cheat requires catching 100 fish.
