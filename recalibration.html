Recalibration Walkthrough: GTA 6 Mission 19

Recalibration is the 19th story mission of GTA 6: beers for Cal, a fishing trip on his boat, and a cookout on his dock. It also teaches fishing — and it hides a point of no return. Read the warning before you start it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Point of no return — do this first. After Recalibration, Jason's house in Key Lento is gone for good, and the pocket cash you carry into the mission is lost. Before starting: make a separate manual save, and put at least $5,000 in Family Savings. You also can't roam as both Jason and Lucia for several hours into Chapter 2 afterwards.

How to complete Recalibration

  1. Buy a six-pack at the liquor store. Any one will do — it's paid for as soon as you take it from the fridge.
  2. Drive to Cal's house. He's on the lounger behind it, by the water.
  3. Take Cal's boat to the fishing spot, about two minutes away by boat.
  4. Fish with Cal until you have a few — three fish, according to PowerPyx. (Fishing controls: hold L2 to grip the rod, R2 to cast and again to strike when a fish bites, left stick to fight it, right stick to reel.)
  5. Back at Cal's dock, grill the fish — flip them before they burn. Cook all three until done on both sides; Cal puts them in buns.
  6. Serve Cal and Lucia. After a while Lucia leaves: follow her out to the front of the house, talk, and take her home.

Reward: none reported. The mission teaches fishing and cooking.
