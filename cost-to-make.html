How Much Did GTA 6 Cost to Make?

GTA 6 is widely reported to be the most expensive game ever made — but nobody outside Take-Two knows the real number. Here is what the estimates say, what they actually cover, and what Rockstar and Take-Two have confirmed.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short answer

Development is estimated at $1–1.5 billion, and the total rises to around $2 billion once marketing and online infrastructure are counted. Take-Two has never disclosed the real budget, and Rockstar's head of publishing has dismissed a rumour that the cost passed $1.5 billion. Every figure below is an estimate — read what each one covers before comparing them, because a development-only number and a development-plus-marketing number are not the same claim.

The figures, and what each one covers

  • $1–1.5 billion — development: the most cited estimate, from industry analysts speaking anonymously to Business Insider in May 2026. It became the standard number after IGN and PC Gamer picked it up. Roth analyst Eric Handler separately put development at "over $1 billion".
  • Not more than ~$1.5 billion: Rockstar's head of publishing, Jennifer Kolbe, dismissed a rumour that the cost passed $1.5 billion (Bloomberg Businessweek, October 2026). That denial rules out the higher rumours; it does not confirm the game cost anywhere near $1.5 billion.
  • Around $2 billion — development + marketing + online infrastructure: the headline figure on YouTube and social media, and the figure Le Temps reports as the development cost. This is the all-in number, not the development-only number.
  • $2.15 billion — capitalized development, ALL unreleased Take-Two games: the only hard figure in the debate, from Take-Two's SEC filing (10-K for fiscal 2026, 31 March 2026). It covers every unreleased game at the company, not GTA 6 alone — so it overstates GTA 6, and it also understates it, because early work and marketing never appear in that line.
  • $3 billion: the upper edge of the rumours, extrapolated from Rockstar North salaries (Spieltimes, March 2026). The $1.5 billion denial above rules this out.

What Rockstar and Take-Two actually said

Almost nothing with a number in it. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick, asked directly by Business Insider in May 2026, called the game "very expensive" and left it there. That is the only on-the-record comment from the company about the budget. The confirmed record from Take-Two's own filings is that development began in earnest in 2020 — which puts the active build at six years by launch, the longest in the series.

How long did it take to make?

  • Pre-production began after GTA V (2013) — early work on the next game started while GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 were still being supported.
  • Development in earnest from 2020 (Take-Two filings) — six years of full production by launch.
  • "Roughly a decade" in production overall, per Bloomberg's reporting — counting the early years as well as full production.
  • About 4,700 developers at Rockstar by August 2026, up from 2,441 in 2018 — an increase of 93 per cent — across 12 offices.

Why did it cost so much?

  • Headcount: nearly 5,000 developers working in multiple countries over 8–10 years is most of the bill — payroll, not technology.
  • Scale: the biggest map Rockstar has ever made — about twice the size of GTA 5's and three times Red Dead Redemption 2's — with two protagonists, 91 story missions and a living world to fill.
  • Detail: a reported 600,000+ animations, compared with 55,000 in GTA 5 and 300,000 in Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Time: two delays (May 2025 and November 2025) pushed the launch from 2025 to 19 November 2026, and every extra month of a team that size costs money.

For comparison: GTA 5

GTA 5 cost about $265 million (development and marketing, 2013 estimate). GTA 6's development estimate alone is roughly four to six times that — which is why it is talked about as the most expensive game ever made, ahead of any film: the two Avengers films shot together cost $898 million to $1.05 billion adjusted for inflation, per ScreenCrush via the New York Post.

Did it make the money back?

Yes, on the first day — by the estimates. Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 earned about $2.36 billion from 24.58 million copies on launch day, which already exceeds even the highest development estimate. That figure is an outside estimate, not a Take-Two announcement — see the full breakdown on our how much money GTA 6 has made page.
