GTA 6 Controls: Default vs Classic Layout

GTA 6 has a new control scheme that modernises the gameplay — run is now on L3 and jump is on Cross/X. If a decade of GTA 5 is in your muscle memory, you can switch back.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Default vs Classic control scheme

The control scheme is set to the new Default layout automatically. To change it: press Options and select Settings > Control Scheme, then toggle to Classic for the GTA 5 approach.

Cheat code entry (phone + D-pad)

Cheats are entered on the in-game phone: press Up on the D-pad to open the phone, hold L1+R1 (PS5) or LB+RB (Xbox), then enter the D-pad sequence. See the full GTA 6 cheat codes list.

Fishing controls

Hold L2 / Left Trigger to grip the rod, R2 / Right Trigger to cast and again to strike when a fish bites, left stick to fight the fish, right stick to reel. See how to fish in GTA 6.

Difficulty assists

GTA 6 has no traditional difficulty options, but under Settings > Gameplay (options 24 and 25) you can enable Accessible Combat (less damage taken, more damage dealt) and Reduced Vehicle Damage.
