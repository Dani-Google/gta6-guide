How to Make Money Fast in GTA 6

The fastest way to make money in GTA 6 depends on your story progress. Better fences and bigger robberies unlock as you advance — so the best method in Chapter 1 is not the best method in Chapter 4.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Don't farm cash in Chapter 1. Farming money in Chapter 1 is pointless — you lose it at the end of the chapter anyway. Just focus on the story, and use small robberies only when you need cash for the next objective.

Chapter 1: small money only

  • Rob stores — aim your gun at the clerk and loot the cash register.
  • Rob gang operations — yellow icons on the map (39 in total across the game).
  • Win races — 40 unique race locations.
  • Underwater treasure for Kenton — he gives you dive spots around Key Lento; sell him the treasures for tens of thousands if you find every piece.

Chapter 2: sell stolen cars to Moses

After the Moses mission (Take A Vehicle to Moses), you can sell stolen cars to Moses in Port Gellhorn. He only accepts cars without trackers. They typically pay $1,000+ for 1–2 minutes of work, with a short cooldown (around 2 minutes) before his garage opens again.

Chapter 3: jewelry heists and Randy

Chapter 3 is where money-making gets efficient — and you get $120,000 automatically from story progress at the end of the chapter. New methods:

  • Jewelry heists — pay up to $25,000.
  • Deliver valuable cars to Randy (western Vice City) — sometimes over $30,000.
  • Sell cars with Standard Trackers for more money than untracked cars.

Chapter 4: bank vault robberies — up to $205,000

The absolute fastest way to make money is robbing the bank vaults of large banks — up to $205,000 for about 5 minutes of work. They unlock at the end of Chapter 4 through the optional quest "That's Where The Money Is…" (Wait at the Cafe), which teaches you the method: follow security vans until they stop at a bank, then enter the vault while it's open. After the quest, Yasmani (the fence from Chapter 3) sells tips about security vans for $10,000 — you must first do his earlier quests (robbing 2 Payday Loans). Robbing a bank vault instantly gives a 4-star wanted level.

Other ways to earn

  • Muggings — knock NPCs down and loot them, or threaten them at gunpoint. Quick pocket change, not a fortune.
  • Gang hideouts — rival gang hideouts (yellow icons) hold illegal goods, cash, and legendary guns and vehicles to steal and sell. Drop goods off with dealers like Tyrone, Dunk or Donnie and get paid almost instantly.
  • Hunting for Bret — Bret of Killtelligence Provisions pays up to a few thousand dollars for a pristine animal skin (one-shot kills keep it pristine). Go for the largest animals only — boars, bears and alligators — so Bret arranges a pickup.
  • Competitions — pool, drone flying, shooting range competitions and races. Usually no more than a few thousand dollars.
  • Collectibles at pawn shops — cards, doubloons and meteorites sell for a few hundred bucks each.
  • Story missions — many pay cash, but not all, so missions alone aren't a reliable income.

One more thing: stolen goods aren't money yet. Jewelry, drugs, marked cash and cars must go to specialised fences, who unlock as the story progresses — and better fences pay more.
