GTA 6 Map: Leonida, Size & Fast Travel

GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida — and it is the biggest map the studio has ever made. Here are the six regions, how big it actually is, and how to get around it.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The six regions of Leonida

Rockstar's official GTA VI site names six major regions, and Game Informer's cover story confirms the same six:

  • Vice City — the neon-lit metropolitan centre, Rockstar's version of Miami. The main city, and where Jason and Lucia's story is centred.
  • Leonida Keys — an island chain off the coast, with a laid-back coastal setting.
  • Grassrivers — a sprawling wetland environment, Leonida's version of the Everglades. Home to alligators, airboats and the hunting grounds.
  • Port Gellhorn — a separate coastal destination. This is where Moses buys stolen cars.
  • Ambrosia — an industrial and commercial region in the centre of the state.
  • Mount Kalaga National Park — a natural landscape of hills and wilderness, built for a different kind of exploration.

Rockstar has not released an official GTA 6 map image — the maps circulating online are fan-made, and county names on them were unconfirmed before launch.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published an official size in square kilometres. What the studio has said, in comparisons, is this — from Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson, relayed by YouTuber TGG after a visit to Rockstar North and reported by Kotaku in August 2026:

  • The whole map is about twice the size of GTA 5's map.
  • About three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's map.
  • Vice City alone is twice the size of Los Santos.
  • Vice City and its surrounding suburbs are 11 times the size of Los Santos and its surroundings.

Rockstar North co-studio head Aaron Garbut told Game Informer (29 September 2026) that Leonida is the biggest map Rockstar has made "by some degree". GameSpot also reported that Rockstar told Edge magazine the total landmass is nearly as big as the playable maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined.

Community measurements disagree with each other, because they count land and water differently:

  • Around 83 km² of explorable land in one community measurement (The Loadout), with mapping communities ranging up to roughly 100 km² of land.
  • 150–160 km² once the ocean is included (Insider Gaming extrapolation, via The Loadout).
  • Roughly 175 km² (16 km × 10.9 km, about 35% water) measured from the released game's full in-game map by PowerPyx (reported 19 November 2026).

For comparison, fan measurements put GTA 5 at about 49 km² of land (roughly 76 km² including water) and Red Dead Redemption 2 at about 36 km² of explorable land. Treat every kilometre figure as an estimate — only Rockstar's ratios are developer statements, and the ratios are the more useful guide until Rockstar says more.

The five counties

In the released game, Leonida is organised into five counties, with the six marketed regions sitting inside them (PowerPyx full world map, 19 November 2026):

  • Mariana — south
  • Vice-Dale — the main city area
  • Kelly — north-west
  • Leonard — north-east
  • Lummox — north

Do regions unlock as you play?

No map-gating system has been confirmed. Rockstar has not described regions as locked behind story progress, and the story itself travels across all six regions from early on — missions take Jason and Lucia from Vice City out to the Keys, Grassrivers and beyond. If any area is gated, it has not been documented in the guides written from the released game so far.

Fast travel

  • RydeMe — a rideshare-style fast travel service reported by one source only (District VI). Treat this as unconfirmed until a second outlet verifies it.
  • Taxis — reviewers finishing the game reported using taxis for faster travel between distant points (IGN playtime roundup).
  • GPS navigation — launch footage confirms the in-game map has turn-by-turn GPS navigation, and the minimap shows the police wanted zone (launch gameplay, verified 19 November 2026).

Driving is still the main way to cross Leonida — and with a map this size, that is a deliberate part of the game. Use fast travel for long hops between regions, and drive when you are hunting, collecting or exploring.
