How Much Money Has GTA 6 Made?

GTA 6 made more money in one day than any game — or film — ever has. But the headline number is an outside estimate, not an official figure. Here is what is actually known, and what is still unconfirmed.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The headline number: $2.36 billion on day one (estimate)

Market research firm Sensor Tower estimates GTA 6 sold 24.58 million copies and earned about $2.36 billion by the end of its launch day, 19 November 2026 — pre-orders included, digital purchases on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That makes it the fastest-selling video game of all time and the biggest entertainment launch in history. The estimate was reported by VGC, GameSpot, Notebookcheck, PSU, TweakTown and others on 19–20 November 2026.

PlayStation vs Xbox split

  • PlayStation 5: 18.44 million copies, $1.77 billion — about 75% of sales.
  • Xbox Series X|S: 6.15 million copies, $588 million — about 25% of sales.

That is roughly a 3-to-1 split in PlayStation's favour.

How the number built up

Sensor Tower tracked pre-orders climbing for weeks before launch:

  • By 13 November: about 12 million pre-orders, worth $1.09 billion.
  • By Tuesday 17 November: $1.54 billion from 15.88 million units.
  • Midday Wednesday (launch eve): $1.65 billion from 17.1 million copies.
  • Thursday morning (launch day): $1.84 billion from 19.1 million copies — already past the $1.8 billion worldwide box office gross of the year's biggest film, The Odyssey.
  • By the end of launch day: $2.36 billion — a further $520 million during the day itself.

Sensor Tower's senior vice president Karl Kontus said pre-order revenue alone had passed $2 billion through launch day, and called GTA 6 "2026's best-selling game based on pre-orders alone".

Records broken

  • GTA 5 (2013): $815.7 million on day one, about 11.2 million copies, and $1 billion in three days. GTA 6 made nearly three times GTA 5's day-one revenue in a single day, and about 2.2 times as many copies.
  • Biggest entertainment launch ever: bigger than any film opening or game launch before it.
  • Fastest-selling game of all time: 24.58 million copies in one day.

What is NOT official yet

Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed any sales figure. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on 19 November that the company will share official sales data soon — so the first hard number will come from Take-Two's own reporting, not from a tracker. Until then, every figure on this page is a Sensor Tower estimate. Treat the $2.36 billion as the best available estimate, not a confirmed total.

What to expect next

  • Launch week: Newzoo projected in July that GTA 6 could book between $3.3 billion and $5.2 billion in launch-week revenue.
  • Take-Two's quarter: Take-Two expects a record $8.1 billion in net bookings in the quarter following GTA 6's release (Q3 FY27).
  • Analyst forecasts: industry analysts projected GTA 6 would generate between $10 billion and $15 billion in revenue overall.

Caveats on the numbers

  • Digital only: Sensor Tower's figures cover digital purchases in Sony's and Microsoft's stores. Boxed copies sold in shops are not included.
  • Refunds not included: the estimates do not subtract refunds.
  • Estimates move: Sensor Tower's own tracker shifted by about $520 million between a morning and evening check on launch day, as more sales data came in.
  • Gross sales, not Take-Two's cut: storefronts take a percentage, so Take-Two keeps less than the headline figure.
