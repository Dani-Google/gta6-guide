GTA 6 Bank Vault Robberies: How to Rob a Bank Vault

Bank vault robberies are the fastest way to make money in GTA 6 — up to $205,000 for about 5 minutes of work. But they only unlock at the very end of the game, and the 100% checklist wants 8 vaults hit.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How to unlock bank vault robberies

Bank vault robberies unlock at the end of Chapter 4, before the ending questline — effectively at the point of no return. Do the optional quest "That's Where The Money Is…" (Wait at the Cafe), which acts as a tutorial: follow security vans until they stop at a bank, then follow the security guard and enter the vault while it's open to carry money inside.

How to rob a bank vault

  1. Complete the "That's Where The Money Is…" quest to learn the method.
  2. After the quest, pay Yasmani — the fence unlocked from story in Chapter 3 — for tips about security vans. Tips cost $10,000. If you haven't done Yasmani's previous quests yet, you must do those first (robbing 2 Payday Loans).
  3. Follow a security van until it stops at a bank, then enter the vault while it's open.
  4. Escape. Robbing a bank vault instantly gives a 4-star wanted level, so plan your getaway first — see how to lose the cops.

For 100% completion: you must steal from 8 vaults, and fully complete 22 bank jobs. Bank jobs and vaults are among the few checklist items that require everything — see the full 100% completion checklist.
