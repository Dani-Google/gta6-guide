How to Heal & Raise Max Health in GTA 6

Your health bar only refills partway on its own in GTA 6. Here's how to heal fully — and how to make the bar longer for good.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How to heal in GTA 6

  • Zombix (fastest in a fight). Hold L1 and press Square on PS5 (LB + X on Xbox). It works mid-gunfight. Zombix is a pill bottle with a heart symbol — find it as story pickups and loot, including on bodies you search after a fight. The game first teaches it in Jason's opening mission, Gone Fishing, where the objective reads "Get the Zombix".
  • Food and drink with a heart icon heal you fully. Protein shakes are in the fridge and protein bars in the kitchen cupboard at Jason's house — all free.
  • Sleeping at your safehouse fills your health completely. The Help menu says sleeping restores health and Focus and gives stamina a temporary buff.

Zombix, first aid kits and adrenaline are the three healing items in the game. Only Zombix goes in your pocket and heals you on command — hold L1/LB to open the weapon wheel and your item slots sit in the bottom-left corner with a count under each one. In a gunfight, get behind cover and let the bar recover what it can first: a fight you start low stays low.

How to raise max health: Strength training

Strength training is the only way to a longer health bar. Each character has three fitness stats — Strength, Cardio and Focus — and Strength is the one that sets the length of your health bar.

  1. Use the weights on the back deck of Jason's house, or any gym. The Help menu names bench presses, chest flies and deadlifts.
  2. Keep lifting until the game tells you the workout is complete and it's time to rest. There is a daily cap.
  3. Drink a protein shake or eat a protein bar afterwards — it cuts the recovery time in half.
  4. Sleep in a bed. The game's own tip is to sleep before and after a workout.
  5. Repeat with the other character. Jason and Lucia train their stats separately.

Jason starts at Strength level 3 of 4, and falling to 1 of 4 costs him roughly a third of his health — Strength can drop if you skip training for weeks. See our full workouts explained guide for the whole fitness system.
