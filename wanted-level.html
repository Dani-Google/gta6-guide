How to Lose the Cops in GTA 6

Escaping the police in GTA 6 is not as simple as driving away and waiting for the stars to disappear. The police remember your appearance, clothing, vehicle and other details — and even after you lose them, the area stays hot.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How the wanted system works

Police respond when a crime is reported — a witness calls them, an alarm goes off during a robbery, or a stolen vehicle with an advanced tracker reports itself. The more serious your offences, the higher your wanted level. Unlike previous games, losing the cops doesn't wipe the slate clean: the police keep information about the crime and use it to recognise you around your last known location.

Wanted star states

GTA 6 has a six-star wanted level. The look of the stars tells you what the police are doing right now:

StarsWhat it meansWhat to do
HollowA crime was reported, but police haven't identified the suspect.Leave the scene immediately — you may avoid a pursuit entirely.
Solid whitePolice can see you and are actively pursuing you.Break their line of sight — alleys, buildings, corners.
Flashing whitePolice lost sight of you and are searching your last known position.Stay hidden and leave the search radius without being spotted.
RedYou escaped the pursuit, but the area stays hot.Leave the hot area. If spotted there, your wanted level comes back. At six stars, the hot area locks down the entire map.

Heat icons: what the police know about you

The red icons beneath the wanted stars show the information police can use to identify you after a crime. Each one must be dealt with separately:

  • Person — a physical description of the suspect.
  • Clothing — what you were wearing. Change your outfit.
  • Vehicle — a description of the vehicle used. Switch cars, or change its paint at a Pay 'n' Spray.
  • Weapon — a weapon was witnessed. Put it away.
  • Two people — Jason and Lucia were identified as a pair. Split up to instantly reduce a level.
  • CCTV camera — security-camera footage of the suspect.

A clothing change addresses the outfit lead only. It does not also replace an identified car or separate a pair of suspects — match your response to the information shown.

How to lose the cops fast

  1. Break line of sight. Turn into narrow streets, drive behind buildings, escape through alleyways. Avoid long open roads where officers can keep tracking you.
  2. Find somewhere to hide. Once police lose sight of you, stop somewhere concealed or keep moving through backstreets.
  3. Change your getaway vehicle — but only after breaking line of sight. Switching cars in front of an officer won't help. Abandon the identified car and switch to another, or respray it.
  4. Change your clothes or appearance — switch outfits, change your hairstyle, use accessories.
  5. Put your weapon away.
  6. Leave the police search area. Open your map and move away from the marked hot area.
  7. Stay out of trouble until the remaining police attention fades.

To slow a pursuit, shoot out cop car tires rather than shooting to kill — and helicopters may retreat if shot at. And remember: cash you carry is lost if you are Wasted or Busted, so bank it at an ATM whenever you can.
