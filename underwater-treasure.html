GTA 6 Underwater Treasure: Kenton's Dive Spots

Kenton runs a scuba-boat tourist shop in Key Lento — and does illegal treasure diving on the side. He needs someone younger to do the diving, and he pays well for what you bring up.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

How underwater treasure diving works

You're introduced to Kenton fairly early in the story. He gives you the approximate locations for treasure dives all along the ocean floor around Key Lento. Take your boat and scuba gear, head out to the dive spots, and find buried treasure under the ocean surface. Hand your finds to Kenton and he pays you for them — an early report says the reward may arrive hours later, after he sells the items.

It might not seem lucrative at first, but Kenton will eventually transfer tens of thousands of dollars if you find every piece of treasure at each dive spot. It's one of the more profitable and fun side activities in GTA 6.

Underwater discoveries and 100% completion

The 100% completion checklist requires 33 of 33 underwater discoveries found — one of the few tasks that needs everything, not a partial count. Kenton sets are separate: you must complete 5 of 7 Kenton sets. See the full 100% completion checklist.

Tip: treasure diving is available from Chapter 1, so it's worth doing between campaign objectives — but like all Chapter 1 money, don't count on keeping a fortune from it. See how to make money fast in GTA 6.
