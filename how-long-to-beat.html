How Long to Beat GTA 6

GTA 6 is a long game — how long depends entirely on how you play it. A rushed run through the story and a completionist run are more than 100 hours apart. Here is what reviewers who actually finished it clocked.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The quick answer

  • Main story, rushed: around 40–50 hours.
  • Main story, typical pace: 50–80 hours. Most reviewers landed at 65–80 hours.
  • Story + side content: 90–120 hours.
  • 100% completion / Platinum: around 200 hours — an estimate. No reviewer had completed 100% by launch day, so this number is not measured yet.

The game has 91 story missions across five chapters, so the story alone is a serious commitment before you touch any side content.

What reviewers actually clocked

IGN's playtime roundup (18 November 2026) recorded four finishes between 42 and 115 hours — every one of them included at least some optional content:

  • Fraser Gilbert (Pure Xbox) — 42 hours: mostly the main story, plus a few hours of side missions, mini golf and free roaming.
  • Chris Tapsell (Eurogamer) — about 65 hours: a focus on the story and character side missions, especially in the final quarter, with some faster travel by taxi.
  • Simon Cardy (IGN) — 70 hours: a story-focused first chapter, then completing available optional missions before moving on.
  • Luke Reilly (IGN) — 115 hours: frequent detours into mini games, exploration and encounters along the way.

Other outlets line up with that range: Polygon's fastest playthrough finished in around 53 hours (still including side quests and exploration) and says to expect 70–80 hours for the main story; GameSpot's team put the main campaign at upwards of 65 hours, with staff reaching 90–100 hours once they explored side missions; VGC's Jordan Middler reached the credits after 100 hours with substantial side content, and estimated around 60 hours for a story-focused run.

What Rockstar said

Rockstar co-studio head Rob Nelson told The New York Times his February playthrough ran about 80 hours — but that run covered the main story plus optional goals with narrative consequences, so it is not a story-only time. He later told Polygon his most recent full-story playthrough took just over 100 hours, in a run he described as "rushed". Rockstar's Scott Mitchell told Radio Times that internal playthroughs ranged from 40–50 hours to 80–100 hours "and some longer", and that the average is hard to gauge because it comes down to player choice.

Why the numbers differ so much

  • Pace: mainlining the story vs stopping for every side mission, mini game and random encounter.
  • Side content: character side missions, Strangers & Freaks-style encounters, races, hunting, fishing, competitions and collectibles all add hours — and the game has a lot of them.
  • Travel: Leonida is about twice the size of GTA 5's map. Driving everywhere instead of using taxis or fast travel adds real time.
  • Money: you need cash for the final missions, so even a story-focused run forces some side activities.

How long is 100% completion?

Around 200 hours, estimated — not measured. PowerPyx's game-length guide (18 November 2026) estimated roughly 200 hours for the 100% checklist and Platinum; its author finished the story in 70 hours with optional missions and all cutscenes. Insider Gaming reported the same ~200-hour figure for the Platinum. But as of 19 November 2026, no playthrough covered in the press had established a measured 100% completion time — GameSpot noted that nobody on its team had completed 100% of the game. One telling detail from IGN's roundup: after the credits, Simon Cardy spent another 30 hours exploring slowly and advanced the checklist by just one percentage point. Treat 200 hours as the best current estimate, and expect it to be revised once players actually finish the checklist.

How GTA 6 compares

  • GTA 5: 30–32 hours main story, 90–100 hours for 100%.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2: 50–60 hours main story, 170–195 hours for 100%.
  • GTA 6: 50–80 hours main story, around 200 hours for 100% (estimated).

GTA 6's story alone is roughly double GTA 5's, and its completionist run is in the same league as Red Dead Redemption 2's.
