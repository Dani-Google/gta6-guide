How Romance Works in GTA 6

Jason and Lucia are in a relationship — but whether they are soulmates or just platonic crime partners is up to you. Their relationship level changes how they interact: in love, they kiss, hug and compliment each other more. Let it die, and they drift apart.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The four relationship levels

LevelSymbolWhat it means
Hot TogetherBurning heartMadly in love, constantly showing affection.
Crazy in LovePink heartIn love.
Partners in CrimePink and gray broken heartMore like platonic partners than romantic partners.
Running on EmptyGray broken heartPlatonic partners.

To check your current level, open the Jason & Lucia menu — one of the four symbols shows at the bottom of the screen. You cannot fully break them up: they always stay partners in crime.

How to boost your relationship in GTA 6

Every time you do something that positively affects the relationship, a pink heart sign appears on the right side of the screen. These actions improve it:

  • Do missions together. Unless it's a solo mission, invite your partner along.
  • Do activities together — bank robberies, carjacking, mini golf, whatever you like.
  • Hold hands while walking together.
  • Greet your partner and use affectionate interactions.
  • Treat your partner. While out together, buy food or drinks and offer them to your partner.

Get a head start in Chapter 1

Before Lucia arrives, Jason can prepare for her: fix the house, buy flowers, and collect Lucia's clothes before picking her up — on time — at the prison gate. Do the optional objectives and you start GTA 6 with a stronger relationship. Skip them and you start lower.

How to ruin your relationship in GTA 6

  • Go alone. Do missions solo whenever teaming up isn't necessary.
  • Refuse to hang out. When your partner asks to meet up, say you don't have time.
  • Ignore your partner. Don't answer their texts and calls.

Rockstar says the strength of Jason and Lucia's bond has some impact on how the overall story plays out — though keeping the romance alive does not guarantee a good ending.
