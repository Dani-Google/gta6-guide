How to Hunt in GTA 6

Hunting in GTA 6 is a money-maker, a 100% completion requirement, and a way to study Leonida's wildlife. Bret of Killtelligence Provisions buys your skins — but only pristine ones pay well.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Bret and Killtelligence Provisions

Bret, the owner of Killtelligence Provisions, pays up to a few thousand dollars for a pristine animal skin. One-shot kills keep a skin pristine. Go for the largest animals only — boars, bears and alligators — so you can call Bret and let him arrange a pickup. If you shoot smaller animals, you have to deliver the skins yourself.

Hunting is too time-consuming to rank among the best money-making methods, but if you're out in the wilds anyway, it's worth shooting some game. See how to make money fast in GTA 6 for the methods that pay better.

Bret's hunting quests and 100% completion

Bret has a questline of 15 hunting quests, and finishing all 15 of Bret's Target Decks is required for 100% completion. Some hunting is still required even though most of the checklist is lenient — the target decks are one of the few tasks that need everything. Studying animals is separate: the checklist wants 100 of 255 animals studied, which you do by looking at them through the binoculars — no hunting required for that part.

Note on criminal profile: hunting counts in the profile system — hurting animals (some hunting counts) pushes you toward Psycho. Keep that in mind if you're trying to stay Professional. See how to change your criminal profile.
