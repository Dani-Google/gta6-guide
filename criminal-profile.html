How to Change Your Criminal Profile in GTA 6

GTA 6 tracks how Jason and Lucia commit crimes. The reputation system is called the criminal profile — and unlike a wanted level, it does not reset when you escape the cops. Rockstar says the choices behind it change how the story develops.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The five criminal profile levels

LevelIconWhat it means
ProfessionalYellowAvoids casualties while committing crime. As close to a good person as GTA 6 gets.
HardenedOrangeDoesn't hesitate to kill, but generally avoids harming people without reason.
ViolentPurpleUses excessive violence against police and civilians.
PsychoGrayExcessive violence for a while — think several deadly robberies in a row.
IrredeemableBlackExcessive violence for a prolonged period. Permanent.

To check your current profile, open the Jason & Lucia menu or hold right on the D-pad. Each character has their own profile, but one character's actions can affect the other when you are out together.

Irredeemable is permanent. Once you cross into it, you cannot come back. Civilians react differently, Jason and Lucia's relationship suffers, police open fire sooner, and you can no longer surrender. If you see a scratched-out icon while at Psycho, stop the chaos right away — that means you are close.

How to improve your criminal profile

The profile works like a scale: the less aggressive your behavior, the more you move toward Professional. Changing it takes time — a couple of low-violence robberies can bring Hardened back to Professional, but a long violent streak takes much longer to undo.

  • Don't hit or shoot civilians — avoid harming anyone without a reason, including fistfights.
  • Don't run people over in traffic — hard while fleeing the police, but don't do it on purpose.
  • Incapacitate instead of killing. In gunfights, aim for knees, elbows, hands and feet. In Focus mode (slowing time), non-lethal weak spots show yellow; avoid the red (lethal) ones.
  • Do clean robberies. Enter banks and stores through a back or side door and avoid detection.
  • Let witnesses go. If a store clerk catches you emptying the safe, let him run and call the police — if you have time to escape with the loot, change your clothes and appearance before the police arrive.

Crimes a mission requires do not count against you — only excess violence does. Self-defence does not count either.

How to worsen your criminal profile

Do the opposite of everything above: drive recklessly, aim for the head, attack cops and civilians, kill defenceless people, and make sure there are witnesses.

Criminal profile and Focus

Your profile also changes how you build Focus (the slow-motion meter):

  • Professional and Hardened: non-lethal takedowns and kind acts toward citizens raise Focus. Petting dogs and cats helps while Professional.
  • Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable: headshots raise Focus. While Violent, headshots also make it harder to return to a lower profile.
