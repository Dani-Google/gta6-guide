GTA 6 Workouts Explained: Strength, Cardio & Focus

Working out is optional in GTA 6 — but it buffs health and stamina, changes how Jason and Lucia look, and Strength training is the only way to raise your maximum health. Here's how the system works.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The three fitness tracks

TrackWhat it doesHow to raise itCan it drop?
Strength (pink)Health — how much damage you can take. Adds bonus health to your health bar.Gym or weight sets: bench presses, chest flies, deadliftsYes, if you miss workout windows
Cardio (green)Stamina — how long you can sprint, swim and cycleTreadmill, cycling, punching bag; running, cycling and swimming also help a littleYes, if you miss workout windows
Focus (purple)How long you can slow time in combatBasketball, fishing, golf, pool, meditation, shooting ranges; non-lethal takedowns or headshots (depends on criminal profile)No — every increase is permanent

Each track has four levels, and each level takes 3 to 4 full workouts to reach. Jason starts the game at Strength level 3 of 4 — you can see it in his health bar: everything to the right of the white dash is bonus health from Strength. You can see all three meters by pressing Down during gameplay, or in the Fitness app on your phone (purple icon), which also shows hunger, tiredness, body composition (muscle and body fat) and whether your workout window is open.

What are workout windows?

Workout windows appear in the Fitness app. They are a period of time where you must complete a Strength or Cardio workout to sustain your fitness and not regress. Fail to train inside the window and your levels slip back — a level 3 Strength can fall to level 2 if you stop training, and skipping training for weeks can drop Jason from Strength 3 to 1, costing roughly a third of his health.

Where to work out

The first weight set is free: on the terrace/back deck of Jason's safehouse in Key Lento. Later you can pay to use fitness studios around the map. Jason and Lucia train separately — a workout with one does nothing for the other, so plan training for both. The exception is training together.

How to get the most out of workouts

  • Exercise after eating — it buffs your Cardio and gives you more stamina to complete your sets. Check if you're Hungry in the Fitness app.
  • Drink a protein shake or eat a protein bar after a workout — it cuts the recovery time in half. Protein shakes are in the fridge and protein bars in the kitchen cupboard at Jason's house, all free.
  • Sleep before and after a workout — the game's own tip is to sleep around training sessions. There is a daily cap on workout gains.
  • Finish every set the game asks for. A workout only pays off if you complete it.
