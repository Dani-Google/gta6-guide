How to Sell Stolen Cars in GTA 6

Carjacking is always a good way to earn cash in GTA 6 — but who you sell to matters. Different buyers pay different prices, and some cars can't be sold to some buyers at all.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Moses — Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2)

Early in Chapter 2, after the main quest Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses), you can sell stolen cars to Moses. He only accepts cars without trackers. These typically pay $1,000+ for 1–2 minutes of work. After selling a car there's a short cooldown (around 2 minutes) before his garage opens again.

Randy — western Vice City (Chapter 3)

Randy hands out vehicle contracts in western Vice City and becomes available after his assignment in Chapter 3. Delivering valuable cars to Randy sometimes pays over $30,000 — far more than Moses. In Chapter 3 you can also sell stolen vehicles with Standard Trackers for more money.

Pay 'n' Spray

You can also drive stolen cars to Pay 'n' Spray shops to sell them. The exact amount varies greatly depending on the type of car. Pay 'n' Spray is also where you respray a car to change its paint — useful after a theft, since the police remember your vehicle's description.

Tips for selling cars

  • Scan first with WAiNK. Hold up on the D-pad and point the app at a car to see its security, tracker status, sell value and registration cost before you steal it.
  • Trackers matter. Moses only takes untracked cars; a stolen vehicle with an advanced tracker can report itself to the police.
  • Vehicle condition affects the payout. Estimates vary — a clean escape and an undamaged car pay better.
  • Fencing counts toward 100%. The checklist requires $200,000 of vehicles fenced. See the full 100% completion checklist.
